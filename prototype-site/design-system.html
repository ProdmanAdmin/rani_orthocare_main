02Who we design for

People in pain, within 15 to 20 km of the clinic

Most visitors arrive worried and in discomfort, on a phone. Many are older, many read Tamil more easily than English, and some read slowly. Every choice below is made for them.

Elderly patient

60+, knee, back or hip pain, may find walking hard. Often reads Tamil first. Weaker eyesight.

  • Big, clear text
  • One obvious step: call
  • Pictures that explain

Family member

25 to 50, booking for a parent, often on a phone, sometimes from another city.

  • Doctor, timings, map, phone in seconds
  • WhatsApp to ask first

Working adult

IT staff, drivers, riders and manual workers with neck or back pain, or wanting prevention.

  • Plain explanation
  • What the clinic will do

Parent of a child

A child with clubfoot, a leg deformity or a bone problem.

  • Reassurance
  • Clear steps and the doctor's details

03Principles

Seven rules every page follows

  1. Clear before clever

    Plain layouts and high contrast. Nothing decorative competes with words.

  2. Readable without glasses

    Body text 18 px on phones, 20 px on desktop. Nothing below 16 px.

  3. One main action

    Call or WhatsApp is always one tap away. Each section ends with one next step.

  4. Words with pictures

    Every service has an icon or photo and a label. Icons never stand alone.

  5. One language at a time

    A page is fully English or fully Tamil, with the switch always visible.

  6. Calm and trustworthy

    Real photos, soft blue structure, green only for "go". No fear imagery.

  7. Same place, every page

    Header, language switch, call buttons and footer never move.

06Why these choices

Fonts and colours that feel like healing

Every choice comes from the patients and from the clinic's own logo. The aim: clear, calm and kind, in both languages.

ENGLISH FONT

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

  • Made for weak eyesight. Commissioned by the Braille Institute of America, a charity for people with low vision. Most of our readers are older.
  • Every letter is unmistakable. A misread phone number, timing or medicine name is a real problem in healthcare. Clear letters are part of patient safety.
  • Warm, not clinical. Rounded, open shapes feel friendly and calm, right for a clinic that promises relief first.

TAMIL FONT

Noto Sans Tamil

  • Familiar. The standard Tamil font on most Android phones, which most of our readers use. Familiar letters are less tiring to read.
  • Complete and correct. Every letter, conjunct and vowel sign is covered, so health words are read exactly as written.
  • Calm and even. Plain strokes keep long Tamil words clear, and match the steady tone of the English face.

Navy and blue

From the blue "R". The colour people most link with medicine, cleanliness and trust. Navy adds quiet expertise. It calms rather than alarms.

Green

From the spine in the "R". Growth, recovery, healing and "go". Kept for one job: the next step towards recovery.

White with soft bands

Clean and hygienic, with soft sky and green bands so every section has its own calm space.

Red only for errors

Red means alarm and pain. The clinic's message is relief, so red appears only on form errors.

The palette tells the care journey in colour: calm blue (you are in safe hands), then green (movement and recovery).

The samples are drawn at the size of the screen you pick. Mobile is under 640 px, tablet 640 to 1023 px, desktop 1024 px and up.

Tamil rules

  • Same sizes as English, with taller line heights so vowel signs above and below never collide.
  • Headings may wrap to 2 or 3 lines. Buttons allow 2 lines of Tamil.
  • No capitals, no letter-spacing, no italics in Tamil.
  • Never set Tamil in an English-only font.

Reading rules

  • 45 to 70 characters per line (max width 68ch).
  • Left aligned, never justified. Centre only short headings.
  • 2 to 4 sentences per paragraph. Lists for steps and symptoms.
  • Phone numbers and timings in aligned figures.

11Checklist

Before any page goes live

Checked at 375 px, 768 px and 1280 px wide, in English and in Tamil.

  • Body text 18 px or more on phones, 20 px on desktop. Nothing under 16 px.
  • Body text contrast 7:1 or more. Buttons and large text 4.5:1 or more.
  • One H1. Headings in order: H1, H2, H3, H4.
  • Every icon has a word. Every image has alt text in the page's language.
  • Tap targets 48 × 48 px or more. Focus ring visible on everything.
  • Works at 200% zoom with no sideways scrolling.
  • Tamil headings and buttons wrap cleanly, with no clipped vowel signs.
  • Call, WhatsApp and Directions one tap away on phones.
  • No information by colour alone. Nothing moves on its own.
  • Calm words only: no "best", "guaranteed", "100%" or "permanent cure".
